# Research image sources

The portrait and `imgs/papers/` figures are retained from the original personal website.

Additional figures are copied from the author's public project repositories and used to illustrate the corresponding papers:

- `imgs/research/vlif-spiking-neuron.png`: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bbc3b477-8125-4416-84e3-604b6e9bd88d (linked by https://github.com/ChrisChen1023/VLIF). Compares traditional LIF and Visual LIF neuron processing.
- `imgs/research/micro-robot-swarm-pipeline.png`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChrisChen1023/Micro-Robot-Swarm/main/img/icra-pipeline.png . Data simulation, coordinate prediction and reconstruction pipeline.
- `imgs/research/sem-net-overview.png`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChrisChen1023/SEM-Net/main/img/sem-overview.png . SEM-Net architecture and SEM block.
- `imgs/research/uiesnn-overview.jpg`: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.08376v1/fig/overview3.jpg . Original Figure 2 from UIESNN, https://arxiv.org/html/2605.08376v1#S1.F2 . Multi-scale spiking residual blocks and frequency decomposition. JPEG, 4241×1800; original bytes preserved.
- `imgs/research/deep-sea-overview.png`: https://arxiv.org/html/2508.12824v1/images/overview9.png . Original Figure 2 from DEEP-SEA, https://arxiv.org/html/2508.12824v1#S2.F2 . Dual-frequency self-attention and spatial-frequency modulation. PNG, 2080×962; original bytes preserved.

No synthetic scientific figures are used. Papers without an available preview use a typographic identifier rather than an unrelated image.

The Research overview carousels show HINT, SEM-Net and DAEVI for computer vision; Visual LIF and UIESNN for brain-inspired AI; and the micro-robot pipeline, DEEP-SEA and UIESNN for robotic perception. UIESNN spans both spiking computation and underwater perception. Each slide shows the complete figure with descriptive alternative text, full-size image access and the corresponding paper link. Figures are contained rather than cropped. Carousel assignments live in `data/research-areas.json`, referencing the canonical publication records.
