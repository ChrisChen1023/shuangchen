Computer vision · Brain-inspired AI · Robotics

Shuang (Chris) Chen.

Postdoctoral Research Associate & Guest Lecturer
Computer Science, Durham University

I study how machines can see, learn and act in the real world. My research connects computer vision, brain-inspired computing and robotic perception, from image restoration to efficient spiking neural networks.

At Durham, I work on autonomous environmental monitoring within BioDiMoBot. My longer-term goal is to translate principles from compact biological systems into artificial neurons and networks for reliable, efficient robotics.

06 / Beyond research

Teaching, mentoring & community.

Learning by building

I teach Computer Vision and Deep Learning at Durham, connecting ideas with practical PyTorch and Jupyter experiments. My teaching includes visual explanations, reproducible experiments and constructive feedback.

I supervise undergraduate projects and contribute to the co-supervision and mentoring of doctoral researchers. Current project themes include multimodal coral identification, SNNs for 3D point clouds and visual world models.

An open academic community

As Durham Computer Science's Postdoctoral Representative, I represent colleagues and help improve access to mentoring. I also review for conferences and journals including CVPR, AAAI, IROS, BMVC and IEEE Transactions on Multimedia.

My outreach includes Sutton Trust Summer School teaching, university open days, and AI and robotics activities with primary- and secondary-school pupils.
