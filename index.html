I study how machines can see, learn and act in the real world. My research connects computer vision, brain-inspired computing and robotic perception.我研究机器如何在真实世界中看、学与行动。我的工作连接计算机视觉、类脑计算与机器人感知。

At Durham, I work on autonomous environmental monitoring within BioDiMoBot. My longer-term goal is to translate principles from compact biological systems into artificial neurons and networks for reliable, efficient robotics.在杜伦，我参与 BioDiMoBot 项目的自主环境监测研究。长远目标是把微小生物系统的原理转化为人工神经元与网络，服务于可靠、高效的机器人。
